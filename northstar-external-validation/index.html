EXTERNAL BLIND VALIDATION

Give NORTHSTAR one failure you already solved. Keep the answer hidden.

Submit a sanitized RAG, citation, retrieval, provenance, or evidence failure. NORTHSTAR receives the trace without the known root cause, predicts where the failure originated, and returns evidence for comparison afterward.

No account. No migration. No confidential data required. Do not include the fix, root cause, credentials, secrets, personal identifiers, or private customer content.

PROTOCOL

A small experiment, not a sales process.

  1. 01

    Sanitize

    Keep pipeline structure, stage transitions, provenance IDs, ordering, mappings, metadata, and observable evidence. Replace sensitive content with placeholders or hashes.

  2. 02

    Hide ground truth

    Do not include the known cause, final fix, incident resolution, remediation, workaround, diagnosis, or postmortem conclusion. That information must stay unavailable until the prediction is sealed.

  3. 03

    Seal the artifact

    The builder calculates a SHA-256 digest of the exact sanitized export. Keep that digest with the trace so the blind prediction can be tied to the same artifact without changing it later.

  4. 04

    Validate blind

    NORTHSTAR predicts the originating and contributing causes, confidence, supporting evidence, and—when possible—a reduced reproducer and counterfactual repair.

PRIVACY FIRST

Preserve causal structure, not sensitive content.

Use placeholders, public sample text, hashes, redacted chunks, synthetic equivalents, or IDs. NORTHSTAR only needs enough structure to reason about the failure path.

Keep

Stage names, timestamps, retrieval ranks, score changes, source IDs, citation mappings, model outputs when safe, and observable symptoms.

Remove

API keys, auth tokens, email addresses, personal data, confidential documents, proprietary source text, customer identifiers, passwords, known diagnosis, root cause, remediation, and fix details.

TRACE BUILDER

Create a leak-resistant northstar_trace.json.

This builder runs locally in your browser. Its Content Security Policy blocks network connections while you prepare the trace. It rejects common ground-truth fields, credential patterns, email addresses, and US SSN patterns before export. These checks are intentionally conservative and cannot replace human review.

Maximum export size: 64 KB. Keys that appear to reveal cause, diagnosis, remediation, fixes, credentials, or secrets are blocked. The builder also rejects several common secret and personal-identifier patterns.

READY TO REVIEW

Sanitized export

Send this digest alongside the sanitized trace. It fingerprints the exact export used for the blind run; changing even one byte produces a different digest.

Final sharing remains deliberate: inspect the file, then send it through the contact route provided to you. Do not append the known answer in the same message. Keep the ground truth separate until NORTHSTAR's prediction has been sealed.

FALLBACK

Need a different trace format?

If your system cannot export this structure, send a redacted structural trace, public reproduction, or a short description plus enough pipeline artifacts to test blind. Keep the known diagnosis separate until scoring is complete.
